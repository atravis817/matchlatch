# MATCHLATCH V1.2 — Studio-First UX Specification
Date: 2026-10-09
Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL — not deployed, not merged.
Baseline: staging/v1.1-tile-cleanup-100926 @ d20c6028a1d8937f1f81d8748d3fd2cf8f7e1fdf

## North star
From inspiration to a personalized, budget-respecting, shoppable outfit with clear product provenance. Make the existing five-destination experience cohesive rather than adding unrelated features.

## Product invariants
- Preserve five destinations: MOOD, MY CLOSET, STUDIO, STORE, ME. STUDIO remains centered in the GreenGlass bottom dock.
- Preserve the exact deep forest-green MATCHLATCH signature, high-contrast white space, purposeful restrained layers, dark-mode parity.
- Preserve the nine-slot outfit tree: hat, scarf, jacket, shirt, watch, belt, pants, socks, shoes. Optional slots may be skipped.
- Do not claim stock, size, final price, shipping, or discounts are confirmed by a web-search citation. A product can be shoppable only after a trusted catalog/retailer verification.
- Merchandise budget must never be exceeded by a proposed swap. Tax/shipping remain clearly excluded from subtotal unless explicitly calculated.
- Preserve owner-scoped private closets, image storage, sign-in, and explicit beta AI gate.
- Do not automatically transmit photos to AI; require user action, consent and valid beta access. No invented checkout integrations.
- All screens keyboard/touch accessible, clear loading/error/empty states, reduced-motion aware.

## Primary user journey
1. MOOD: Browse or choose an inspiration, or start from photo/description.
2. STUDIO: Recognize source item (AI if opted in, otherwise guided); show current style / size / occasion / budget with quick temporary overrides.
3. STUDIO: Compose and refine visible look, one item slot at a time. Show required pieces and optional pieces, reason for recommendation, confidence/provenance.
4. STORE: Hand off context-preserving filters to verified catalog. Separate an opt-in Explore Wider Web area explicitly labeled research-only.
5. MY CLOSET: Save complete look and source inspiration into an optional named collection; return with selected items and budget intact.

## Milestone A — Journey cohesion
Acceptance:
- One primary CTA per screen; no dead-end navigation.
- From source inspiration to returned Studio look without re-entering profile.
- Swap one piece without resetting other pieces; Back restores selection, budget and scroll context.
- Save & resume on same device, with a clear guest/account state.
- No new primary tabs or parallel cart.

## Milestone B — Personal intelligence
Acceptance:
- Sizing, color, material exclusions, fit, climate, occasion and budget are strong constraints; temporary overrides do not silently overwrite saved profile.
- Explain the matching rationale in one short sentence; decline to generate certainty about unverified inventory.
- AI failures return to guided styling without data loss, no automatic paid retrials.
- Compare consistent evaluation prompts and responses across at least ten representative style scenarios.

## Milestone C — Shopping confidence
Acceptance:
- Real provider-origin variants only, with retailer name, image, currency, price timestamp, explicit available signal, size, and merchant URL.
- Exact variant reverified before retailer handoff; no automatic checkout assertion.
- Clear visual distinction: 'Verified catalog listing' vs 'Web reference — research only'.
- All replacements recalculate exact subtotal, refuse any over-cap change, and preserve previous choice.
- Awin products remain unpublished until joined membership, compliant feed and approval gate verified.

## Milestone D — GreenGlass and usability
Acceptance:
- Forest green is recognizable in light/dark, with adequate text contrast and distinguishable focus indicators.
- Bottom dock is readable, doesn't obscure important controls on iPhone Safari, respects safe-area inset; Studio remains centered.
- Screen-reader labels for image upload, swap arrows, saving, budget updates, and source citations.
- User testing on iPhone Safari and desktop: no horizontal overflow, disabled actions explained, quick loading and error recovery.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Release gates / blockers
P0: Vercel preview credentials for OpenAI (preview-scoped only, never committed), actual API smoke calls, Shopify catalog returns and fallback, owner isolation and signed-in device checks, automated contract tests.
P1: Retailer feed permission and import, crawler public routes, accessibility sweep, merchant link attribution.
Do not promote preview to production until these pass and specific staging SHA is approved.
